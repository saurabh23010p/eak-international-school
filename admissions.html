Every child's journey is different. We welcome families who want a supportive environment for learning, discovery and personal growth.

A simple admission process

Our sample five-step process helps families understand what to expect.

STEP 01

Enquiry

Learn about the school, available grades and admission requirements.

STEP 02

Application

Complete the application and prepare the required documents.

STEP 03

Interaction / Assessment

Attend an age-appropriate interaction or assessment, where applicable.

STEP 04

Selection

The school reviews the application and communicates the next steps.

STEP 05

Enrolment

Complete the remaining formalities after confirmation from the school.

Eligibility and grade placement

Admission depends on the grade applied for, the child's age and the school's admission policy.

  • Parents should confirm the age criteria for the requested grade.
  • Previous school records may be needed for higher classes.
  • Admission is subject to eligibility and seat availability.

This is a website prototype. Please confirm actual eligibility rules with the school.

Documents to prepare

Student records

Birth certificate and recent photographs, if required.

Academic records

Previous report card and transfer certificate, where applicable.

Parent / Guardian details

Contact information and address proof, as requested by the school.

The final document list should be confirmed with the school office.

Important dates

Admission enquiries are welcome.

Contact the school office to confirm application dates, assessment schedules and seat availability. Exact dates have not been published in this prototype.

Frequently asked questions

How do I begin the admission process?

Start with an enquiry, confirm grade eligibility and follow the application instructions shared by the school.

Which documents should I bring?

Prepare student identity and academic records as applicable. Confirm the final list with the school office.

Is an assessment required?

Requirements may vary by grade. The school will explain the applicable process during the enquiry.

Can I submit an enquiry online?

This demonstration page does not send information to a server. Use the school's verified contact details for a real enquiry.
